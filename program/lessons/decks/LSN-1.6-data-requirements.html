Read the shape

Nearly a straight line against a log x-axis: every 10× more labelled data buys a roughly constant number of points — and costs ten times as much to label. That is the economics of every supervised proposal VSP will write.

Count the outcome, not the rows

12,000 photos ÷ 14 milestone types = 857 each if evenly spread, and they never are. A client with a milestone that occurs on 40 photos has 40 examples, not 12,000. GAP-5: placeholder volumes

And the metric stops working

LSN-0.3: 100 real cases inside 10,098 alerts → 0.98% precision, one real alert per 102. LSN-1.3: a model that flags nothing scored 99.20% accuracy. Rare outcome + accuracy = a number that cannot fail and cannot help.

The honest upside, in the same breath: every point on that line was trained from scratch. Nobody starts from scratch on images any more — starting from a network already trained on millions of photographs shifts the whole curve left. What does not change is the shape.
