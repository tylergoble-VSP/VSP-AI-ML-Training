Why .T is there — and why it is the bug

nn.Linear stores weights as (out, in) — 128 rows of 784 — so it can compute x @ W.T. Shape confusion lives at exactly this boundary. Which is why the shape-debug ritual is a rehearsed move, not an improvisation.

Where the "non-linearity" actually is

For this image 48 of the 128 hidden units came out negative and ReLU set them to exactly zero — so 80 units did all the work. Different image, different 80. That switching is the whole of it. Without it, two stacked Linears collapse into one.

Why this is a sales asset, not a "no"

Every point above was trained from scratch — and nobody in industry does that on images any more. That is a real, sellable answer to "we only have 200 photos" — and it is the same trade-off you meet again in LSN-2.5.
