The Cadence

Pre-work isn't homework.
It's the price of a seat.

We watched a good trainer lose 90% of a VSP audience in the first 20% of a course — because nobody had done intentional prior work. This program is designed so that never happens again.

Before — pre-work

Mandatory, in every lesson plan — 45 min today. You arrive with an artifact: a question, an error message, a screenshot. No pre-work, no seat.

During — session

Direction, not hand-holding. Every session is 1 h, plus 2 h out of session — locked. Hands-on lessons are live labs; drills use real client scenarios.

After — homework

Hands-on lessons produce a graded artifact — a trained model, a working RAG, an agent with traces. Artifacts are your checkpoint evidence.

Today's pre-work was the venv setup. A broken install is a valid artifact — that's what the next 25 minutes are for.
