The Cadence

Pre-work isn't homework.
It's the price of a seat.

Before — pre-work

  • Mandatory, in every lesson plan — 45 min today
  • Arrive with an artifact: a question, an error, a screenshot
  • No pre-work, no seat

During — session

  • Direction, not hand-holding
  • 1 h live + 2 h out of session — locked
  • Hands-on lessons are live labs; drills use client scenarios

After — homework

  • Hands-on lessons produce a graded artifact
  • A trained model, a working RAG, an agent with traces
  • Artifacts are your checkpoint evidence
Today's pre-work was the venv setup. A broken install is a valid artifact — that's what the next 25 minutes are for.
